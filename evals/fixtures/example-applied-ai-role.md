# Example role input for evaluation: Applied AI Scientist, Generative AI

## Mission

The role focuses on exploring and applying emerging Generative AI technologies to create useful products, capabilities, and experiences. It combines experimentation, application development, and technical innovation, with an emphasis on turning promising ideas into practical solutions.

## Core responsibilities

The scientist designs applications, workflows, agents, and intelligent systems and evolves them from concept toward production. The role improves quality, reliability, and effectiveness through testing and iteration. It also collaborates with scientists, engineers, and product teams and contributes to reliable, high-quality, and responsible AI solutions.

## Required profile

The role values hands-on experience with modern AI models, platforms, and tools. Relevant concepts include retrieval-augmented generation, agentic workflows, tool use, model evaluation, and AI orchestration patterns. It also requires software-development judgment, problem solving, independent project ownership, curiosity, adaptability, communication, and cross-functional collaboration.

## Additional advantages

Helpful evidence includes AI-powered products used in production, machine learning or analytical experimentation, open-source or technical-community contributions, hackathons, research initiatives, personal AI projects, large-scale data, analytics, and complex systems.
